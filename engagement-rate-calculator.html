Engagement Rate Calculator — FAQs

What is a good engagement rate?

By followers: above 6% is excellent, 3–6% good, 1–3% average. By reach, rates run higher — above 10% is excellent. Rates vary by platform and account size.

Should I use followers or reach?

By reach is more accurate — it measures people who actually saw the post. By followers is the classic benchmark most reports quote.

How is engagement rate calculated?

(Likes + comments + shares) ÷ followers (or reach) × 100. Enter all three interaction types for the truest number.

Which platforms does this work for?

Any social platform — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, X, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts.
