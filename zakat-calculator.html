Zakat Calculator — FAQs

How is zakat calculated?

Add all zakatable assets (cash, gold, silver, business stock), subtract short-term debts, and if the total meets the nisab threshold, pay 2.5% of it.

What is the nisab threshold?

Nisab is the minimum wealth that makes zakat due: the value of 87.48 grams of gold or 612.36 grams of silver. The default here uses an adjustable estimate — check your local current price.

Are debts deducted?

Short-term, immediately payable debts are deducted from your assets before checking nisab. Long-term debts are generally handled per scholarly guidance — consult a scholar for your situation.

Is gold taxed on its full market value?

Yes — gold and silver held for wealth are zakatable at their current market value on your zakat date, whether jewellery, coins or bars.
