Body Fat Calculator — FAQs

How does the US Navy body fat method work?

It estimates body fat from circumference measurements — height, neck and waist for men, plus hips for women — using logarithmic formulas developed from hydrostatic weighing data.

How accurate is the US Navy method?

Typically within 3–4% of DEXA scan results for most people. Very muscular or very lean individuals may see larger errors.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

For men, 14–24% is generally healthy; for women, 21–31%. Athletes run lower. Essential fat is about 3–5% for men and 10–13% for women.

Why is there a different formula for women?

Women store fat differently (more around hips and thighs), so the female formula includes hip circumference to account for that distribution.
