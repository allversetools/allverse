Metronome — FAQs

Is this metronome accurate?

Yes — it schedules clicks on the Web Audio clock with a look-ahead timer, so beats stay sample-accurate and never drift like old setInterval timers.

What is tap tempo?

Tap the Tap button (or press T) in time with any song and the metronome detects its BPM automatically from your taps.

Which time signatures are supported?

2/4 through 7/8, plus quarter, eighth, triplet and sixteenth subdivisions — with an accent on beat 1 you can toggle.
