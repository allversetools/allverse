Plot Area Converter — FAQs

What is the standard marla in Pakistan?

The revenue-record (official) marla is 272.25 sq ft. 20 marla make 1 kanal (5,445 sq ft) and 8 kanal make 1 acre (43,560 sq ft). Some housing societies measure plots at 225 or 250 sq ft per marla — select the matching standard above.

How many marla are in one kanal and one acre?

1 kanal = 20 marla exactly. 1 acre = 8 kanal = 160 marla. 1 murabba (a traditional farm block) = 25 acres = 200 kanal.

What is a sarsai?

A sarsai (square karam) is 1/9 of a marla — about 30.25 sq ft with the standard marla. Small plots are often quoted as marla + sarsai.
