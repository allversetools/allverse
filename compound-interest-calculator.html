Compound Interest Calculator — FAQs

How is compound interest calculated?

A = P(1 + r/n)^(nt), where P is the principal, r the annual rate, n the compounding frequency per year and t the years. Interest earns interest on itself each period.

Does more frequent compounding earn more money?

Yes, but the difference is small. Monthly compounding on the same rate earns slightly more than yearly compounding because interest starts earning its own interest sooner.

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the principal. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus accumulated interest, so money grows exponentially over time.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a mathematical illustration only. Actual investment returns vary — always consult a qualified financial advisor before investing.
