CPC Calculator — FAQs

What is the difference between CPC and CPM?

CPC (cost per click) = spend ÷ clicks — what each visitor costs. CPM (cost per mille) = spend ÷ impressions × 1000 — what 1,000 views cost.

What is a good CPC?

Under $0.50 is excellent, under $1 good, under $2 average. It varies hugely by industry — legal and finance CPCs can run $5+.

What is a good CPM?

Under $5 is excellent, under $10 good, under $20 average. Social feeds typically run cheaper than search or premium placements.

How can I lower my CPC?

Improve ad relevance and quality score, tighten targeting, test better creatives and landing pages — higher click-through rates directly lower CPC.
