Cooking Unit Converter — FAQs

Why do I need to pick an ingredient for cups to grams?

Cups measure volume and grams measure weight, so the answer depends on the ingredient’s density. A cup of flour weighs about 125g, while a cup of honey weighs about 340g — this converter handles that for you.

Which cup size does the converter use?

The US customary cup of 236.6 ml, the standard in most international recipes. A tablespoon is 15 ml and a teaspoon is 5 ml.

How accurate are the ingredient densities?

They are standard culinary averages, good for everyday cooking. For precise baking, always weigh ingredients on a kitchen scale.
