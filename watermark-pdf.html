How to use

  1. Select a PDF file
  2. Choose text or image, then position, transparency and rotation
  3. Click Add Watermark and download the stamped PDF

Why use this tool?

  • Your PDF never leaves your computer — fully private
  • Text or image watermarks with mosaic repeat
  • Watermark is drawn over the content, like a stamp

FAQs

Does the watermark sit over or under the content?

Over the content, like a stamp — that is how a visible watermark works.

Can I watermark only some pages?

Yes — type page numbers or ranges like 1, 3-5, or leave "all".

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Watermarking happens entirely in your browser.

Does it change my original file?

No — the tool creates a new stamped PDF.
