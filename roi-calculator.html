ROI Calculator — FAQs

What is the difference between ROI and ROAS?

ROI = (revenue − spend) ÷ spend × 100, the percentage profit on your spend. ROAS = revenue ÷ spend, the revenue multiple — e.g. 4.0x means $4 back per $1 spent.

What is a good ROAS?

4.0x+ is excellent, 3.0x+ is good, 2.0x is average, and below 1.0x you are losing money. Benchmarks vary by industry and margins.

How is profit calculated here?

Simply revenue minus ad spend. It does not include product costs, fees or taxes — add those separately for true net profit.
