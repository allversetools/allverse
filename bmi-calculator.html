BMI Calculator — FAQs

How is BMI calculated?

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)². Enter your height and weight to get your result instantly.

Is this BMI calculator accurate?

It uses the standard WHO BMI formula. It is an indicator, not a medical diagnosis.

Is my data stored?

No — everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is saved or sent.
