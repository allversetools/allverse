Ingredient Substitution — FAQs

What can I use instead of an egg in baking?

For one egg use 1 tbsp ground flaxseed + 3 tbsp water (rest 10 minutes), ¼ cup mashed banana, or ¼ cup unsweetened applesauce — best in muffins, pancakes and quick breads.

How do I make buttermilk at home?

Add 1 tablespoon of lemon juice or white vinegar to a measuring cup, fill with milk to 1 cup, and rest for 5 minutes. Or simply use 1 cup of plain yogurt instead.

Do substitutions change the recipe?

Slightly — a substitute can alter flavour, texture or rise, but still gives an acceptable result. Avoid swapping in delicate recipes like soufflés or macarons.
