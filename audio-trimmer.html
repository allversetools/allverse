Audio Trimmer — FAQs

Is the Audio Trimmer really free?

Yes — free, no signup, no watermark and no file-size games. Trim as many files as you like.

Are my audio files uploaded anywhere?

No. Your file is decoded and processed locally with the Web Audio API — nothing ever leaves your device.

What formats can I import and export?

Import MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC and anything your browser can play. Export is studio-quality 16-bit WAV.
