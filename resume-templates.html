Resume Templates — FAQs

Are these resume templates free?

Yes — preview all layouts free and use any of them in the Resume Builder with no account and no watermarks.

Which template is best for ATS?

The Minimal (ATS) template: single column, standard headings, no graphics — the safest format for applicant tracking systems.

Yes — click "Use in Resume Builder" under any template to open the builder with that layout style selected, then fill in your details.
