Flight Duration Calculator — FAQs

How is the flight time estimated?

Using the great-circle (shortest) distance between the two airports divided by a typical cruise speed, plus a taxi/climb/descent buffer. Real flights vary with winds, routing and air traffic.

Which cruise speed should I pick?

Short-haul (~750 km/h) for regional jets, medium (~820 km/h) for typical A320/B737 flights, long-haul (~900 km/h) for wide-body jets like the A350 or 777.

Does it account for time zones?

Yes — arrival is shown in the destination's local time using standard UTC offsets, with a +1d badge when you land the next day. Daylight saving is not applied.
