Income Tax Calculator Pakistan — FAQs

What is the tax-free salary limit in Pakistan for FY 2026-27?

Salaried income up to Rs 600,000 per year (Rs 50,000 per month) is completely tax-free in FY 2026-27.

How is the effective tax rate different from my slab rate?

Your slab rate applies only to the last rupee you earn. The effective rate is total tax ÷ total salary, and it is always lower. For example, a Rs 200,000/month salary has a 20% marginal rate but only about 6.5% effective rate.

Does this calculator include medical allowance or zakat deductions?

No. It applies the FBR salaried slab table to your gross salary. Deductions, allowances and rebates depend on your employer's payroll setup.

Is the 9% surcharge still applicable to high salaries?

No. The Finance Act 2026 removed the 9% surcharge on salaried individuals, even for annual incomes above Rs 10 million.
