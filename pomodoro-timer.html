Pomodoro Timer — FAQs

What is the Pomodoro Technique?

Work in focused 25-minute sprints separated by 5-minute breaks. After four sprints, take a longer 15–30 minute break. It fights fatigue and procrastination.

Can I change the timer lengths?

Yes — set any focus length (1–180 min) and break length (1–60 min), then press Start.

Does it keep running if I switch tabs?

Mostly — the timer uses your system clock so it stays accurate, but sound alerts need the tab open. Keep it in a visible tab.

What sound plays at phase changes?

A short chime when focus ends and a different chime when a break ends, generated in your browser — no audio files needed.
