Random Number Generator — FAQs

What does "unique only" mean?

Every number appears at most once — useful for draws, raffles and lottery-style picks. The count cannot exceed the size of the range in this mode.

Are the numbers truly random?

They come from your browser\u2019s cryptographic-quality random generator — fine for games, draws and everyday use, but not for cryptography or gambling.
