Wedding Expense Splitter — FAQs

How does the settle-up calculation work?

Each expense adds what a person paid and subtracts their share. Net balances are then matched with a greedy minimum-cash-flow algorithm — the largest debtor pays the largest creditor — producing the fewest transfers needed (at most n−1 for n people).

Can I split an expense among only some people?

Yes. When adding an expense, untick anyone who should not share it — for example, only the groom's side for the barat dinner. Their balance is unaffected.

Is my data saved?

Yes, in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded. Use Clear all to wipe the plan, or Mark paid as money actually moves.
