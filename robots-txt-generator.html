Robots.txt Generator — FAQs

What is a robots.txt file?

It is a small text file at your website's root (example.com/robots.txt) that tells search engine crawlers which pages they may or may not crawl.

What is the difference between Allow and Disallow?

Disallow blocks crawlers from a path (e.g. /admin/), while Allow makes an exception inside a blocked path. Rules are matched top to bottom.

What does crawl-delay do?

It asks polite crawlers to wait a number of seconds between requests, reducing server load. Note that Googlebot ignores crawl-delay — use Search Console's crawl settings for Google.

Where do I upload robots.txt?

Upload it to the root of your domain so it is reachable at https://yourdomain.com/robots.txt. Then verify it in Google Search Console.
