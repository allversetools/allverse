Wedding Budget Planner — FAQs

How should a Pakistani wedding budget be split?

A common starting split: venue 20%, catering 28–30%, decor 10%, photography 10%, clothes 8%, makeup 5%, music 5%, and a 10–14% misc buffer. Move the sliders to match your priorities — the tool warns you if catering falls below your guest count's need.

Why is catering calculated per head?

In Pakistan catering is quoted per guest (per head), so total food cost = guests × per-head rate. Enter your expected guest count and the caterer's per-head quote to see the real number instantly.

Will my plan be saved?

Yes — the Save button stores your budget, guest count and slider positions in your browser (localStorage). It reloads automatically next visit; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
