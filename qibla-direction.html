Qibla Direction — FAQs

How do I use the Qibla compass?

Tap "Enable Compass", allow the motion-sensor permission, hold your phone flat, and rotate until the green Qibla arrow points straight up. Calibrate by moving your phone in a figure-8 if the needle jumps.

Why does it need sensor permission?

iPhones and some Android browsers require an explicit tap to grant access to the compass sensor. Without it the page cannot read your phone's orientation.

What if my device has no compass sensor?

Use the manual fallback: pick your city to see the fixed Qibla bearing in degrees clockwise from true north (approximate), measured against the Kaaba in Makkah.

Magnetic north or true north?

Phone compasses usually read magnetic north, which in Pakistan differs from true north by a few degrees. For exact salah alignment, cross-check with your mosque or a local prayer timetable.
