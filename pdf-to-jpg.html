FAQs

How many JPGs will I get?

One JPG per page — a 5-page PDF gives you 5 JPG files.

What quality should I pick?

Normal suits sharing and documents. High gives larger, sharper images for print or zooming.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.

Can I extract embedded images instead?

This tool converts whole pages to JPG. Embedded-image extraction is not included.
