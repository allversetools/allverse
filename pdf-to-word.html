PDF to Word

Convert your PDF to an editable Word document online — text and basic formatting preserved, free in your browser.

Everything happens in your browser — your file never leaves your device.

Text and basic formatting (bold, size) are preserved. Complex layouts, tables and images are converted to plain text — layout is approximated, not pixel-perfect.

How to use

  1. Select a PDF file
  2. Click Convert to Word
  3. Download the editable DOCX file

Why use this tool?

  • Your PDF never leaves your computer — fully private
  • Text, bold and font sizes carried over
  • Each PDF page can start on a new Word page

FAQs

Will the layout look exactly like the PDF?

No — this is a text-based conversion. Words, paragraphs, bold and sizes are preserved, but complex layouts, tables and images become plain text. For exact layout, keep the PDF.

Are images converted?

No — images in the PDF are not carried into the Word file. Only text is extracted.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser.

What if my PDF is scanned (no text)?

Scanned pages have no text layer, so they convert to empty pages. A scanned PDF needs OCR first, which this tool does not do.
