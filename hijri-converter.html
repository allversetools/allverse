Hijri Converter — FAQs

How accurate is the Hijri conversion?

Hijri dates depend on actual moon sighting, which can shift a date by ±1 day versus calendar calculation. This tool uses the arithmetic Islamic calendar as an approximation — always confirm Ramadan, Eid and other occasions with your local mosque.

Which direction conversions are supported?

Both: Gregorian to Hijri, and Hijri to Gregorian.

Why is the Hijri year shorter?

The Islamic calendar is lunar: a year is about 354 days, so Hijri dates move about 11 days earlier each Gregorian year.

What if my browser does not support the Islamic calendar?

Modern browsers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) support the Islamic calendar. If yours does not, the page shows a graceful message suggesting you update your browser.
