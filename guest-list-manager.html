Guest List Manager — FAQs

How are per-event headcounts calculated?

Each guest counts as 1 plus their plus-ones, added to every event they attend. Declined guests are excluded automatically, so your caterer and venue get the true number per function.

Can I track RSVP status?

Yes — each guest is Invited, Confirmed or Declined, changeable anytime from the list. The headcount panel shows confirmed vs pending totals at a glance.

Can I share the list with my caterer?

Yes — Copy list puts a formatted per-event list on your clipboard, and Download .txt saves it as a file you can WhatsApp or email.
