Resume Builder — FAQs

Is this resume builder really free?

Yes — build unlimited resumes, print or save them as PDF, with no account, no watermarks and no paywall.

Will my resume pass applicant tracking systems (ATS)?

The layouts are single-column with standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills) and common fonts — the format ATS parsers read most reliably. Run the finished resume through our ATS Resume Checker to score it.

Is my personal data private?

Completely. Your resume is built locally in your browser. Drafts are saved only in your own browser's storage — nothing is uploaded anywhere.
