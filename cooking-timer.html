Cooking Timer — FAQs

Can I run several timers at the same time?

Yes — add as many named timers as you like (pasta, eggs, cake…) and start, pause or reset each one independently.

Do the timers keep working if I switch tabs?

Yes. Timers are driven by the clock rather than the screen, so they stay accurate even when the tab is in the background.

How do I hear the alarm?

Each finished timer plays a loud repeating beep through your device speakers. Keep the tab open and your volume up — test the sound first with the “Test sound” button.
