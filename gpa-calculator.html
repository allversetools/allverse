GPA Calculator — FAQs

What is the 4.0 GPA scale?

A+ and A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0. Your GPA is the credit-weighted average.

How is GPA calculated?

Each course's grade points are multiplied by its credits; the total is divided by total credits. Heavier courses count more.

Is this weighted or unweighted GPA?

Unweighted — the standard 4.0 scale. Honors/AP weighting (e.g. +0.5 or +1.0) is not applied.

Is my data saved anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is stored or sent.
