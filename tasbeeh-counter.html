Tasbeeh Counter — FAQs

How do I use the tasbeeh counter?

Tap the big button (or press the spacebar) once per dhikr. The count, progress ring and cycles update automatically.

What targets can I set?

Presets of 33, 100, 500 and 1000, plus any custom number you enter.

Does the count save if I close the page?

No — the count lives only while the page is open, keeping it private. Reloading resets it.

What is the vibration feedback?

On phones that support it, the device buzzes lightly every 33 counts and longer when you reach your target.
