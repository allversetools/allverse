Readability Checker — FAQs

What is the Flesch Reading Ease score?

A 0–100 scale measuring how easy text is to read. Higher scores mean easier reading; 60–70 is plain English most adults understand.

What is a good Flesch-Kincaid grade level?

It estimates the US school grade needed to understand the text. Grade 7–9 works for a general audience; academic writing often lands at grade 12+.

How are syllables counted?

With a vowel-group heuristic in your browser — each group of vowels counts as one syllable, with silent endings adjusted. It is about 95% accurate for English.

Is my text kept private?

Yes. All analysis runs locally in your browser — your text is never uploaded or stored.
