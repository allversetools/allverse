Travel Budget Planner — FAQs

How are the budget estimates calculated?

From researched per-day benchmarks per travel style (budget, mid-range, luxury), scaled by your days and travelers. Every amount is editable, so adjust to real prices you find.

Which currencies are supported?

USD, PKR, EUR, GBP, INR, AED, SAR and CAD with approximate static conversion rates — switch anytime and all amounts convert instantly.

Is my budget plan saved?

Yes — your inputs and edited amounts auto-save in your browser, and you can copy a text summary to share with travel companions.
