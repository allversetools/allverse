Discount Calculator — FAQs

How do I calculate a discount price?

Multiply the price by the discount percent and divide by 100, then subtract from the price. A 25% discount on $80 saves $20 — you pay $60.

How do I find the discount percentage from two prices?

Subtract the sale price from the original price, divide by the original price and multiply by 100. $100 down to $75 is a 25% discount.

Are stacked discounts added together?

No — they apply one after another. 20% off followed by an extra 10% off is not 30% off; it is 28% off the original price.
