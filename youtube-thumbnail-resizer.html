How to use

  1. Pick an image (or drag & drop it).
  2. Choose fill (crops to exact 16:9) or fit (adds letterbox bars).
  3. Download — ready to upload to YouTube Studio.

Why 1280 × 720?

YouTube recommends 1280 × 720 px (16:9) thumbnails under 2 MB. This tool outputs exactly that size.

YouTube Thumbnail Resizer — FAQs

What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

YouTube recommends 1280×720 pixels (16:9). This tool resizes any image to exactly that size.

Is this thumbnail resizer free?

Yes — free, no signup, and your images never leave your browser.

Which formats can I upload?

JPG, PNG, WebP and GIF all work, and you download a crisp 1280×720 thumbnail.
