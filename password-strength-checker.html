Password Strength Checker — FAQs

Is it safe to type my real password here?

Yes. The strength check runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent, stored or logged anywhere. But as a habit, test passwords similar to yours rather than the exact one.

What does the crack-time estimate mean?

It estimates how long a brute-force attack (10 billion guesses per second) would need, based on your password\u2019s length and character variety. Longer, more varied passwords take exponentially longer.

What makes a strong password?

At least 12 characters with a mix of upper and lowercase letters, digits and symbols — unique for every site, ideally stored in a password manager.

Why is length more important than complexity?

Each extra character multiplies the number of possible combinations. A long passphrase like four random words is both stronger and easier to remember than a short complex password.
