Font Pairing — FAQs

What makes a good font pairing?

Contrast in structure with harmony in proportion — e.g. a serif heading with a sans-serif body that share a similar x-height. Never pair two fonts from the same sub-class, and stick to two or three families max.

Are these fonts really free?

Yes — every pairing uses free, open-licensed Google Fonts, and each card gives you a copy-paste embed code.

Can I use these fonts commercially?

Yes. All Google Fonts use the SIL Open Font License, which allows personal and commercial use.
