Electricity Bill Calculator Pakistan — FAQs

What is the difference between protected and unprotected consumers?

A domestic consumer whose 6-month average stays at or below 200 units/month is 'protected' and pays subsidized rates. Cross 200 units and all units are billed at the higher unprotected slab rates.

Why is my actual LESCO bill higher than this estimate?

This estimate covers only variable energy charges plus the Rs 75 fixed charge. Your real bill adds fuel price adjustment (FPA), quarterly tariff adjustment, TV licence fee, electricity duty and sales tax.

What is the lifeline tariff?

Consumers using up to 50 units pay just Rs 3.95/unit and those between 51–100 units pay Rs 7.74/unit — Pakistan's subsidized lifeline rates.

Do three-phase and single-phase connections have the same rates?

The per-unit slab rates are the same; the fixed monthly charge differs (Rs 75 single-phase vs Rs 150 three-phase).
