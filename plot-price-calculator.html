Plot Price Calculator — FAQs

How is plot price calculated from a per-marla rate?

Multiply the per-marla rate by the number of marla. A 10-marla plot at Rs 15 lakh per marla = Rs 1.5 crore. If your rate is per kanal or per sq ft, convert the area to the same unit first — this tool does it automatically.

What does lakh and crore mean?

1 lakh = Rs 100,000 and 1 crore = Rs 100,000,00 (100 lakh). Property in Pakistan is almost always quoted in lakh/crore, so totals here use that format.

Does this include transfer taxes?

No — this is the plot price only. Budget an extra 5–8% for stamp duty, registration, mutation and FBR advance tax (varies by province and filer status).
