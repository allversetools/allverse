Voice Recorder — FAQs

Is the Voice Recorder really free?

Yes — 100% free with no signup. Each take can run up to 30 minutes, and you can record as many takes as you like.

Where do my recordings go? Is it private?

Nowhere. Recording, playback and WAV conversion all happen locally in your browser — your audio is never uploaded to any server.

Which formats can I download?

Your recording's native format (WebM with Opus audio in Chrome/Edge, M4A in Safari), plus one-click conversion to universal 16-bit WAV.
