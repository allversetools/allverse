Video Metadata Viewer — FAQs

Is this video metadata viewer free?

Yes — inspect unlimited videos with no account and no uploads.

How is the FPS measured?

Browsers don't expose a video's exact frame rate, so the tool briefly plays the video and counts presented frames with requestVideoFrameCallback — an estimate accurate to about ±1 FPS.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. All metadata is read locally in your browser — your video never leaves your device.
