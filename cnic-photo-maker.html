CNIC Photo Maker — FAQs

What size should a CNIC or passport photo be in Pakistan?

The standard is 35 × 45 mm on a white background. This tool exports exactly that at 300 DPI (413 × 531 px), ready for print.

Can I use a selfie for a passport photo?

Yes, if it is front-facing with a neutral expression, taken against a plain white wall, with even lighting and no shadows, filters or sunglasses.

Can this tool remove or change my photo's background?

No — it only crops, positions and resizes. Take the photo against a plain white/light background first.

What about the US visa 51×51 mm photo?

The US visa needs a square 2×2 inch photo. This 35×45 mm crop will not match — use a dedicated 2×2 inch crop for US visa applications.
