How is this calculated?

Par score = Team 1 score × (Team 2 resources ÷ Team 1 resources). Resources come from a simplified table built on overs remaining and wickets lost — a full uninterrupted innings counts as 100%. One run above par wins; par itself is a tie.

DLS Par Score Calculator — FAQs

What is the DLS par score?

The par score is the total Team 2 must have reached when rain stops play to be tied on the Duckworth-Lewis-Stern method. One run more wins the match; one run less loses it.

Is this the official ICC DLS calculation?

No. Official matches use the ICC’s proprietary DLS Professional Edition software. This tool uses a simplified resource-table approximation — typically within a few runs — built for fans, clubs and gully cricket.

What are “resources” in DLS?

Resources combine the two things a batting side has left: overs remaining and wickets in hand. A full uninterrupted innings is 100% of resources; rain or lost wickets reduce that percentage.
