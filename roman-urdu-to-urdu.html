Roman Urdu to Urdu Converter — FAQs

How accurate is the Roman Urdu to Urdu conversion?

Very good for everyday text: the built-in dictionary covers 230+ common words and their correct Urdu spellings. Unusual or misspelled words fall back to letter-by-letter transliteration, which is close but not always perfect.

Why does 'main' become میں?

In Pakistani Roman Urdu, 'main' almost always means the pronoun میں. The context-free converter picks the most common meaning — proofread if you meant something else.

Does it handle numbers and punctuation?

Yes — digits, punctuation and spaces are left untouched; only Latin-script words are converted, so 'aaj 10 baje' becomes 'آج 10 بجے'.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser's JavaScript. Nothing you type leaves your device.
