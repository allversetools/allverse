Pregnancy Due Date Calculator — FAQs

How is the due date calculated?

Using Naegele's rule: add 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last period, then adjust by the difference between your cycle length and 28 days.

What is Naegele's rule?

A standard obstetric formula that assumes ovulation on day 14 of a 28-day cycle. It estimates the due date as LMP + 9 months + 7 days.

How accurate is the due date?

Only about 5% of babies arrive on their due date. A first-trimester ultrasound gives a more precise estimate — always confirm with your doctor.

When does the third trimester start?

The third trimester begins at week 28 and runs until delivery, typically around week 40.
