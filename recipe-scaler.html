Recipe Scaler

Cooking for 2 or 20? Paste your ingredients and get perfectly scaled quantities with kitchen-friendly fractions.

Scaled ingredients

    Tip: scaling changes quantities only — baking time, temperature and pan size rarely scale linearly. For very large batches, reduce leavening slightly and check doneness early.

    Recipe Scaler — FAQs

    How do I scale a recipe to different servings?

    Enter the original servings and how many you want to make, paste your ingredients one per line, and the scaler multiplies every quantity — rounding to kitchen-friendly fractions like ½ and ¼.

    What ingredient format should I use?

    One ingredient per line starting with the quantity, for example “2 cups flour”, “1/2 tsp salt” or “3 eggs”. Lines without a quantity, like “salt to taste”, are kept exactly as written.

    Does scaling change cooking time?

    No — this tool scales ingredient quantities only. Baking time and temperature rarely scale linearly, so check for doneness and adjust your pans as needed.
