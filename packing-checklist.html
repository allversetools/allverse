Packing Checklist — FAQs

How does the smart packing list work?

Choose your trip type, duration and weather — the tool builds a categorized checklist with smart quantities (e.g. shirts per day) plus trip-specific gear.

Is my packing list saved?

Yes — your list, checks and custom items are auto-saved in your browser (localStorage), so you can close the tab and come back.

Can I add my own items and print?

Yes. Add custom items to any category, remove ones you don't need, and print the finished list with one click.
