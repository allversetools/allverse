EMI Calculator — FAQs

How is EMI calculated?

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1], where P is the loan amount, R the monthly interest rate and N the tenure in months.

Does a longer tenure reduce my EMI?

Yes, but you pay more total interest over time. This calculator shows both the monthly EMI and the total interest so you can find the right balance.

What is the difference between flat and reducing interest rates?

Most loans use a reducing-balance rate (interest charged on the outstanding amount). A 'flat' rate quote looks lower but costs more — always compare using the EMI.
