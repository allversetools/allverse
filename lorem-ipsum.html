Lorem Ipsum Generator — FAQs

What is lorem ipsum?

Scrambled Latin-style placeholder text designers have used since the 1500s to mock up layouts. It looks like real copy without distracting readers with meaningful words.

Can I use lorem ipsum commercially?

Yes — lorem ipsum is public domain. Use it freely in mockups, templates and client presentations.

Why use placeholder text instead of real copy?

It lets you judge fonts, spacing and layout honestly — real words pull attention to the content instead of the design.
