Video to GIF — FAQs

Is this video to GIF converter free?

Yes — convert unlimited clips with no account, no watermarks and no file-size tricks.

Why does my GIF look lower quality than the video?

The GIF format only supports 256 colors per frame, so some banding is unavoidable. For best results keep clips under 6 seconds at 480px wide and 10 FPS — that is the sweet spot for size vs quality.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

No. Frames are captured and encoded entirely in your browser — your video never leaves your device.
