Water Intake Calculator — FAQs

How much water should I drink per day?

A common guideline is about 35 ml per kilogram of body weight, plus extra for exercise and hot weather. This calculator personalizes that formula for you.

Does tea or coffee count toward water intake?

Yes, mostly — caffeinated drinks still hydrate you, though water is best. Fruits, vegetables and soups also contribute meaningful fluid.

What are signs I'm not drinking enough?

Dark yellow urine, headaches, fatigue, dry mouth and dizziness are common early signs of dehydration.

Can you drink too much water?

Yes — drinking extreme amounts very quickly can cause water intoxication (hyponatremia). Stick near your calculated goal and drink to thirst during exercise.
