Percentage Calculator — FAQs

How do I calculate X percent of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = (20 ÷ 100) × 150 = 30. This tool does it instantly as you type.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. Example: 45 is (45 ÷ 150) × 100 = 30% of 150.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, and multiply by 100. Going from 80 to 100 is a 25% increase.
