Prayer Times — FAQs

How accurate are these prayer times?

The times shown are approximations for October 2026. Prayer times shift every day with the sun, so always confirm with your local mosque before relying on them.

Which cities are supported?

Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar and Quetta.

How does the next-prayer countdown work?

The page reads your device clock and highlights the first prayer whose time has not passed yet, with a live countdown. After Isha it rolls over to tomorrow's Fajr.

Do I need internet for the countdown?

No. The timetable is built into the page and the countdown runs fully offline in your browser.
