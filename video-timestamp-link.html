YouTube Timestamp Link — FAQs

How do YouTube timestamp links work?

A timestamp link adds ?t= (seconds) to the video URL — e.g. youtu.be/abc123?t=90 starts at 1:30. Anyone opening it starts watching at that exact moment.

What are the YouTube chapter rules?

Chapters need at least 3 timestamps in ascending order, the first at 0:00, and each chapter at least 10 seconds long. The formatter above checks all three rules.

Do timestamp links work on mobile?

Yes — tapping a timestamp link opens the YouTube app (or mobile browser) and starts playback at that exact second on both iOS and Android.
