Fancy Text Generator — FAQs

Will fancy text work on Instagram and WhatsApp?

Yes — it is plain Unicode text, so it pastes into bios, captions, comments and chats on Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp, Discord and games. A few exotic styles may render slightly differently on very old phones.

Do I need to install any font to use it?

No. Unicode styled characters display with the fonts already on your device — that is why they work everywhere without setup.
