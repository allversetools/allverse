SIP Calculator — FAQs

What is a SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan lets you invest a fixed amount every month into a mutual fund. It builds wealth through disciplined investing and the power of compounding.

How is SIP maturity calculated?

FV = M × [((1+r)^n − 1) / r] × (1+r), where M is the monthly amount, r the monthly return rate and n the total number of months.

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns depend on market performance. This calculator projects based on your assumed rate — actual results will differ.

What return rate should I assume?

Equity mutual funds in India have historically returned around 10–12% annually over long periods, but past performance never guarantees future results.
