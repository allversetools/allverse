Freelance Rate Calculator — FAQs

How is my hourly rate calculated?

Your target annual income is divided by your total billable hours (hours per week × weeks per year), then a buffer is added for taxes, holidays and non-billable work.

What should I enter for billable hours?

Only the hours you can actually charge clients — exclude admin, marketing, learning and breaks. Most freelancers bill 15–30 hours a week.

What is the buffer for?

Taxes, sick days, holidays, late payments and unpaid admin time. A 20–30% buffer keeps your real income on target.

Is this calculator free?

Yes — free, no signup, everything calculated in your browser.
