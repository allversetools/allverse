Citation Generator — FAQs

Which citation formats are supported?

APA 7th edition and MLA 9th edition, for both websites and books.

How should I enter the author name?

Either “Last, First” or “First Last” works — e.g. “Khan, Ali” or “Ali Khan”. The generator formats it correctly for each style.

What if there is no author?

Leave the author field empty — the citation will start with the title, as both styles require.

Is this citation generator free?

Yes, completely free with no limits and no signup. Always double-check citations against your institution's style guide for critical work.
