Trig functions use degrees. You can also type with your keyboard — Enter for =, Backspace to delete, Esc to clear.

Scientific Calculator — FAQs

Are trig functions in degrees or radians?

Degrees — sin(30) = 0.5. This matches most classroom calculators.

What does the % key do?

It converts the number before it to a percentage — 10% becomes 0.1. So 200 + 10% gives 200.1.

Which functions are supported?

sin, cos, tan (degrees), log (base 10), ln (natural log), square root, squares, arbitrary powers, π and e, plus parentheses and standard operators.

Does it work offline?

Yes — once the page loads, all calculations run in your browser with no internet needed.
