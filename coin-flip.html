Coin Flip — FAQs

Is the coin flip truly random?

Yes — each flip uses your browser\u2019s built-in random number generator with a 50/50 chance for heads or tails.

Can I use this to settle a decision?

Absolutely — that is what it is for. The score tracker keeps count if you flip best-of-3 or best-of-5.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, the 3D coin animation works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Is it free?

Yes — flip as many times as you like, free, no signup.
