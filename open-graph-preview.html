Open Graph Preview — FAQs

What is Open Graph?

Open Graph is a set of meta tags (og:title, og:description, og:image) that control how your link looks when shared on Facebook, WhatsApp, LinkedIn and other platforms.

Why is my preview image not showing on Facebook?

Facebook caches link data. After changing tags, re-scrape your URL with Facebook's Sharing Debugger. Also make sure the image is publicly accessible and at least 1200×630 px.

What is the best image size for link cards?

1200×630 pixels works best for Facebook and WhatsApp; X (Twitter) large cards use 1200×628. Keep the file under 5 MB.

Does this tool post to social media?

No — it only shows a visual mockup in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or shared anywhere.
