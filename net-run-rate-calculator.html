Net Run Rate Calculator — FAQs

What is net run rate (NRR)?

Net run rate is the average runs per over a team scores minus the average runs per over it concedes. It is the standard tie-breaker when teams finish level on points in a tournament.

What happens to NRR if a team is bowled out?

Under ICC rules, a team that is bowled out is treated as having faced its full quota of overs (for example 20 overs in a T20), even if it was dismissed earlier. This calculator applies that rule automatically when you enter 10 wickets lost.

Do no-result or abandoned matches count?

No. Only matches that produce a result — including results decided by the DLS method — count towards net run rate. Uncheck “Count this match” for abandoned or no-result games.
