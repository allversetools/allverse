How to play

MergeMind is a small number puzzle that fits on one screen. You start with two tiles on a four-by-four board. Every move slides every tile as far as it can go in the direction you choose. When two tiles carrying the same number collide, they merge into one tile worth double, and the merged value is added to your score. After each move a new tile appears — usually a 2, sometimes a 4 — so the board slowly fills up. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match. Your goal is simple to state and hard to master: keep merging, keep space open, and chase the 2048 tile.

Controls

Play with your keyboard, your finger, or the on-screen pad. Press the arrow keys or W, A, S, D to slide the board. On a phone or tablet, swipe in the direction you want the tiles to move. Prefer buttons? Tap the arrow pad under the board — each button is large enough to hit without looking. The Undo button steps back one move when a slide goes wrong, and Restart starts a fresh board at any time.

Tips

Work toward one corner and keep your biggest tile there. Build a chain of descending values away from that corner, so a 512 sits beside a 256, a 256 beside a 128, and so on. Avoid sliding your big tile away from its corner unless a merge is waiting. Leave at least one row or column flexible so you can always escape a crowded board. Do not chase every small merge — a merge that traps your largest tile costs more than it pays. Finally, use Undo like a lifeline, not a habit: one step back fixes a slip, but the board still has to be solved by you.

MergeMind — FAQs

What is the goal of MergeMind?

Slide matching number tiles together so they merge into bigger tiles, and keep going until you build the 2048 tile. You can keep playing after reaching 2048 and push your score higher.

Is MergeMind free to play?

Yes. MergeMind is completely free on AllVerse. There is no signup, no download, and no limit on how many games you play.

Can I undo a move in MergeMind?

Yes. The Undo button steps back exactly one move, restoring the board and score from before your last slide. Use it when a slide goes wrong.

Does MergeMind work on mobile?

Yes. Play with swipe gestures on a phone or tablet, or use the on-screen arrow buttons. On a computer you can use the arrow keys or W, A, S, D.

How does the daily target work?

Each day MergeMind sets a fresh score target of 512 or 1024. Reach the target before the game ends to keep your daily streak going.
