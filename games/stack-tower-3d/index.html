How to play Stack Tower 3D

Stack Tower 3D is a one-tap stacking puzzle with a simple, ruthless rule: whatever overhangs the block below falls away. You start with a solid base block. The next block slides back and forth above the tower, first along one axis, then the other, alternating every level. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it. The part that lands on the tower stays; the overhanging part tumbles off into the void and is gone for good. Every imperfect drop makes the tower a little narrower, and a narrower tower is a harder target.

The escape hatch is the perfect drop. Land a block almost exactly on top of the one below and it snaps into place with a flash — no trimming, no narrowing, and your combo grows. Perfects are worth bonus points, and a long perfect streak is how the biggest scores happen. The sliding blocks also get faster as the tower grows, so the timing window tightens the higher you climb. The game ends when a block misses the tower completely and falls away with nothing left to stand on.

Your best tower height is saved on this device. Each day has a blocks mission: stack that many blocks in a single tower to keep your daily streak going.

Tips to build taller

  • Drop at the centre, not the edge. Aim for the middle of the block below — an edge drop always trims the tower.
  • Learn the rhythm. The block slides at a steady pace. Count the beat and drop on the centre beat, not when it looks close.
  • Chase perfects early. A perfect keeps the tower wide, and a wide tower makes every later drop easier.
  • Watch the axis. The slide direction alternates every level — check which way the block is moving before you commit.
  • Stay calm as it speeds up. Later blocks move faster, but the centre is still the centre. Do not rush your tap.
  • Small trims are fine. Losing a sliver is better than missing entirely — a safe drop beats a greedy one.
