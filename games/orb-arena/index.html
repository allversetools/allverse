How to play Orb Arena

Orb Arena drops you into a big square arena as a small orb among ten bot rivals, all hungry. Glowing pellets are scattered everywhere — each one you touch adds mass and makes you a little bigger. Rival orbs work the same way: if you are at least 10% bigger than another orb and you overlap it, you eat it and gain a chunk of its mass. But the rule cuts both ways. Anything bigger than you can eat you, and the bots know it — big ones hunt, small ones run.

Your orb follows your mouse pointer, or you can steer with WASD or the arrow keys, or drag on a touch screen. Bigger orbs move more slowly, so the longer you survive, the harder it is to catch prey — and the harder it is to escape the giants. The arena has walls: use them to corner small orbs, but never let a bigger orb pin you against one. Reach 500 mass and you become Arena Champion and the run ends in glory. Get eaten first, and your score is the peak mass you reached.

The live leaderboard in the corner always shows the top five orbs in the arena, so you know exactly who to chase and who to avoid. Your best mass is saved on this device, and each day has a mass mission: reach it in a single run to keep your daily streak going.

Tips to top the arena

  • Graze pellets first. Pellets are risk-free mass. Grow to a safe size before hunting anyone.
  • Hunt down, never up. Only chase orbs clearly smaller than you. If it looks close in size, it is not worth the risk.
  • Watch the leaderboard. The top orb is usually the biggest threat — keep it on screen or keep away from it.
  • Use your speed while small. Small orbs are fast. Use that speed to dart between pellets and escape hunters.
  • Corner your prey. A smaller orb with a wall behind it has nowhere to run.
  • Never get pinned. If a bigger orb is between you and the open arena, slip sideways along the wall, not deeper into the corner.

Orb Arena — FAQs

Is Orb Arena free to play?

Yes — Orb Arena is completely free, runs in your browser and needs no download or signup.

How do I move in Orb Arena?

On a computer, move your mouse — your orb follows the pointer — or use WASD / arrow keys. On touch screens, drag anywhere to steer.

When can I eat another orb?

You can eat a rival orb when you are at least 10% bigger than it and overlapping it. Bigger orbs can eat you the same way, so keep your distance from anything larger.

How do I win in Orb Arena?

Reach 500 mass to become Arena Champion. If a bigger orb eats you first, the run ends — your score is your peak mass.

Does Orb Arena work on mobile?

Yes — it works on phones, tablets and desktops, with drag-to-steer touch controls.
