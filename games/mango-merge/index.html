How to play

Move your mouse or finger to aim the fruit at the top of the jar, then click or tap to drop it. When two fruits of the same kind touch, they merge into the next bigger fruit and you score points. The chain runs from Berry up through Plum, Lemon, Orange, Mango, Papaya and Melon, all the way to Watermelon — the biggest fruit in the game. Bigger merges pay more points, so a late-game Papaya merge is worth far more than an early Berry merge. Watch the dashed line near the top of the jar: if your pile stays above that line for more than two seconds, the round ends. The trick is to keep the pile low and balanced. Drop small fruits into gaps so matching pairs can roll together, and save the middle of the jar for your biggest fruit. Every round is different because the fruit order is random, so no two games play out the same way.

Controls

On desktop, move the mouse to aim and click to drop. On a phone or tablet, drag to aim and tap to drop. Arrow keys also aim, and the Space key drops the fruit. Use the Pause button any time you need a break, Restart to start a fresh round, and the Sound button to mute the merge pops. The Fullscreen button expands the game for a bigger board.

Tips to score higher

First, keep matching fruit close together — two Berries on opposite sides of the jar will never meet. Second, build in layers: small fruit on top, big fruit at the bottom, so heavy fruit settles instead of rolling over the pile. Third, do not drop the next fruit blindly; check the Next preview and plan one move ahead, because a Berry dropped onto a Melon just adds height. Fourth, use the jar walls: a gentle drop against the side lets a fruit roll into a gap instead of bouncing across the pile. Finally, chase chains. When a merge creates a fruit that touches its twin, it merges again for free, and those chain reactions are where the big scores come from. Patience beats speed — one careful drop is worth five rushed ones.

Mango Merge — FAQs

How do fruits merge in Mango Merge?

When two fruits of the same kind touch, they combine into the next bigger fruit in the chain and you earn points. Berry becomes Plum, Plum becomes Lemon, and so on up to Watermelon.

How do I lose a game of Mango Merge?

If the stack stays above the dashed overflow line for more than 2 seconds, the game ends. Keep the pile low by merging often and dropping new fruit into gaps.

What is the daily challenge?

Every day there is a target score. Beat it to grow your play streak. The target changes daily, so you get a fresh goal each time you visit.

Can I play Mango Merge on my phone?

Yes — drag to aim and tap to drop. The game runs in your browser on phones, tablets and desktops with no download.

Is Mango Merge free?

Yes — it is completely free to play, with no signup and no download.
