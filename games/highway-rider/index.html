How to play Highway Rider

Highway Rider puts you behind the wheel on a busy three-lane highway. Your car drives itself forward — your job is to pick the gaps. Traffic cars travel slower than you in all three lanes, sometimes two abreast, so you are always reading the road ahead: which lane opens up, which gap closes, and where the next coin line runs. A coin line usually marks a lane that stays clear for a few seconds, so following the coins is often the safest line through a busy stretch.

Skimming past a traffic car in the next lane without touching it earns a near-miss bonus of 50 points, multiplied by your combo. The combo grows with every near-miss and resets when you crash, so a brave, clean run scores far more than a cautious one. Coins add 10 points each. The longer you survive, the faster the highway gets and the denser the traffic becomes. Hold boost to go faster and rack up distance quickly — but remember that everything arrives sooner when you are boosting, so only boost when the road ahead is clear.

Your best score is saved on this device. Beat a best of 500 to unlock the Sunset skin and 1500 for the Midnight skin, then pick your favourite on the start screen. Each day has a distance mission: hit it in a single run to keep your daily streak going.

Tips to score higher

  • Follow the coins. A coin line usually runs through a lane that stays open — treat it as a hint, not just points.
  • Chain near-misses. Each near-miss raises your combo multiplier, so skimming two cars in a row pays more than two separate dodges.
  • Boost on straights only. Boost when you can see clear road ahead; never boost into a wall of traffic.
  • Change lanes early. Late swerves cause most crashes — decide your lane two cars ahead, not one.
  • Use all three lanes. The middle lane is a trap when traffic bunches; the outer lanes often hide the longest gaps.
  • Chase the daily mission. A calm, steady run beats a greedy one when the mission distance is on the line.

Highway Rider — FAQs

Is Highway Rider free to play?

Yes — Highway Rider is completely free, runs in your browser and needs no download or signup.

How do I steer in Highway Rider?

On a keyboard use the Left/Right arrow keys or A/D to change lanes, and hold Up/W or Shift to boost. On touch screens, swipe left/right or tap the on-screen arrows, and hold BOOST to go faster.

How do near-miss bonuses work?

Passing a traffic car in the next lane without touching it earns a near-miss bonus of 50 points. Chaining near-misses without crashing builds your combo multiplier.

How do I unlock car skins?

Skins unlock from your best score on this device: the second skin at a best of 500, the third at a best of 1500. Locked skins show their requirement on the start screen.

Does Highway Rider work on mobile?

Yes — it works on phones, tablets and desktops, with swipe controls and tap buttons on touch screens.
