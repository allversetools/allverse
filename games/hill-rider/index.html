How to play Hill Rider

Hill Rider is a physics bike game on an endless rolling landscape. Your bike starts at the foot of the first hill with a full tank. Hold the throttle to climb: the engine pulls you forward, gravity pulls you back, and every crest launches you into the air. In the air you control the tilt — nose up or nose down — and the landing decides everything. Land on both wheels, roughly level with the slope, and you keep rolling. Land tilted too far and you wipe out, ending the run.

Fuel is your other enemy. The tank drains whenever you hold the throttle, and it never refills on its own. Red fuel cans sit along the route, usually just past a tricky crest or a big jump — each one tops the tank back up. Coins are worth 10 points each, distance adds up as you ride, and a full 360-degree flip in the air, landed cleanly, earns a 100-point flip bonus. The hills get steeper the further you go, so a run that felt easy at 200 metres gets genuinely demanding past 1,000.

Each day has a distance mission: reach it in a single run to keep your daily streak going. Your best score is saved on this device, so every ride is a fresh shot at beating yourself.

Tips to ride further

  • Feather the throttle. Full throttle on every slope wastes fuel. Short bursts up the steep parts go further per tank.
  • Level out before landing. Match the slope as you come down — a level landing is a safe landing.
  • Flip only off big crests. Small jumps do not give you enough air time for a full rotation.
  • Chase the fuel cans. A can is worth more than any coin — never jump past one on purpose.
  • Brake on drops. Coming down too fast into the next uphill launches you nose-first into the slope.
  • Watch the fuel gauge. If the tank is low and no can is in sight, ease off and coast to save fuel.

Hill Rider — FAQs

Is Hill Rider free to play?

Yes — Hill Rider is completely free, runs in your browser and needs no download or signup.

How do I control the bike in Hill Rider?

On a keyboard hold Right arrow or D for throttle and Left arrow or A for brake. In the air, the same keys tilt the bike. On touch screens, hold the GAS and BRAKE buttons.

How do flips work?

When the bike is airborne, hold a tilt key to rotate. Complete a full 360-degree flip in the air and land on both wheels to earn a 100-point flip bonus.

What happens when I run out of fuel?

Fuel drains while you hold the throttle. When it empties, the bike coasts to a stop and the run ends — so grab every fuel can you see.

Does Hill Rider work on mobile?

Yes — it works on phones, tablets and desktops, with large GAS and BRAKE touch buttons.
