How to play SkyRush 3D

SkyRush 3D is a free endless runner set on a lively South Asian street, full of painted buildings, chai stalls and buzzing bazaar traffic. Your little auto-rickshaw drives itself forward — your job is to keep it in one piece. The street has three lanes, and trouble arrives in three shapes. Low wooden barriers sit across a lane: jump clean over them. Hanging banners stretch overhead: slide underneath before you reach them. Tall crate stacks block a lane completely: switch lanes early, because no jump or slide will save you there.

Golden gems line the road. Every gem adds points, and a full line of gems usually marks the safest path through the next stretch. The longer you survive, the faster the street rushes at you, so each run gets harder the further you go. One crash ends the run — unless you picked up a shield. Your best score is saved on this device, so every run is a fresh shot at beating yourself.

Tips to score higher

  • Follow the gems. A gem line almost always runs through a free lane — treat it as a hint, not just points.
  • Move early, not late. Switch lanes as soon as you see a tall crate stack. Late swerves are the most common way runs end.
  • Jump late, slide early. A late jump clears a low barrier with room to spare; an early slide keeps you low under a banner.
  • Save the shield. Grab a shield before a crowded stretch — it turns one fatal crash into a second chance.
  • Chase the magnet. While the magnet is active, drift toward gem lines you would normally skip. Each gem is worth 25 points.
  • Beat the mission. Every day has a distance target. A calm, steady run beats a greedy one when the mission is on the line.

SkyRush 3D — FAQs

Is SkyRush 3D free to play?

Yes — SkyRush 3D is completely free, runs in your browser and needs no download or signup.

What are the controls for SkyRush 3D?

On a keyboard use the arrow keys or A/D to switch lanes, Up/W/Space to jump and Down/S to slide. On touch screens, swipe or use the on-screen buttons.

How do magnet and shield power-ups work?

The magnet pulls nearby gems toward your rickshaw for 6 seconds. The shield absorbs one crash, then disappears.

What is the daily mission?

Each day brings a new distance target. Beat it in a single run to keep your daily streak going.

Does SkyRush 3D work on mobile?

Yes — it works on phones, tablets and desktops, with swipe controls and tap buttons on touch screens.
