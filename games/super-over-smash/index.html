How to play Super Over Smash

Super Over Smash is a six-ball cricket chase with fictional teams: the Falcons bat, the Strikers set the target. Every over starts with a fresh target — usually between 12 and 20 runs — and six balls to get there. The bowler runs in and delivers, the ball travels down the pitch, and you swing at exactly the right moment. The ball flashes through a golden hitting zone right in front of the bat: swing while it is there and you are in business.

Timing is everything. A perfectly timed swing launches a six. A good swing, just a touch early or late, finds the boundary for four. A mistimed swing still scrambles one or two runs. And if you do not swing at all, a normal delivery is a dot ball — no harm, no run. The exception is the yorker: the bowler fires it fast and full, the screen warns you with a red flash, and if you miss it or swing late, you are bowled and the over ends immediately.

Win the over and your win count grows. Your best score is saved on this device, and each day brings a fixed daily target: beat it to keep your daily streak going. All teams and players in this game are fictional — no real leagues, teams or players appear.

Tips to win the chase

  • Watch the ball, not the bowler. The ball's speed changes every delivery — trust your eyes, not the rhythm of the last ball.
  • Wait for the golden zone. Swinging early is the most common mistake. Let the ball come to the bat.
  • Respect the yorker. When the red warning flashes, swing a fraction earlier than usual — or accept the dot and protect your wicket.
  • Do the maths. Needing 4 off 3 balls? Singles win it. Needing 12 off 2? Only boundaries do — swing big.
  • Bank the sixes early. A six off the first ball takes all the pressure off the rest of the over.
  • Use the last ball wisely. If one run wins it, a mistimed nudge is enough — do not swing for the stands.

Super Over Smash — FAQs

Is Super Over Smash free to play?

Yes — Super Over Smash is completely free, runs in your browser and needs no download or signup.

How do I hit the ball in Super Over Smash?

Press Space, tap the HIT button, or click the pitch just as the ball reaches the bat. Perfect timing smashes a six, good timing a four, and a mistimed swing scores 1 or 2 runs.

What happens if I miss the ball?

If you do not swing at a normal delivery, it is a dot ball worth 0 runs. A yorker is different: miss it or swing late and you are bowled out, ending the over.

How do I win a super over?

Score at least the target before the 6 balls run out. If you reach the target early, the over ends immediately and you win.

Does Super Over Smash work on mobile?

Yes — it works on phones, tablets and desktops, with a big HIT button on touch screens.
