How to play

WordSprint Daily gives you one hidden 5-letter word and six chances to find it. Type any five letters and press Enter. Each tile flips over to reveal a colour: green means the letter sits in exactly the right spot, amber means the letter is in the word but belongs somewhere else, and grey means the letter does not appear in the word at all. Read the colours, cross off the greys, shuffle the ambers, and lock in the greens. The daily puzzle is the same word for everyone, so you can compare scores with friends — and if you want more, practice mode deals a fresh word every time you ask for one.

Controls

On a computer, just type the letters on your physical keyboard, use Backspace to fix a slip, and press Enter to submit a guess. On a phone or tablet, tap the on-screen keyboard under the board — the keys are large enough to hit without looking, with Enter and Backspace at the ends of the bottom row. Switch between Daily puzzle and Practice with the two tabs above the board, and use New practice word whenever you want a fresh challenge.

Tips

Open with a guess that covers the most common letters. A word packed with everyday consonants and vowels tells you more than a word full of rare letters. After the first flip, treat grey letters as deleted — never spend another guess on them. Amber letters are pure gold: keep them in play and try them in a new position each round. When you have two or three greens, stop guessing at random and think about which letters can still fit the empty slots. And remember that a repeated letter only lights up twice if the hidden word really holds it twice — if your guess repeats a letter and only one tile colours up, the word holds exactly one.

WordSprint Daily — FAQs

How do I play WordSprint Daily?

Type any 5-letter guess and press Enter. Green means the letter is in the word and in the right spot, amber means it is in the word but in the wrong spot, and grey means it is not in the word at all. You get 6 tries to find the word.

Is WordSprint Daily free?

Yes. WordSprint Daily is completely free on AllVerse. There is no signup, no download, and no limit on practice games.

What do the green, amber and grey tiles mean?

Green means the letter is correct and in the right position. Amber means the letter is in the word but in a different position. Grey means the letter does not appear in the word at all.

Can I play more than one puzzle a day?

Yes. Everyone gets one shared daily puzzle, and practice mode gives you unlimited extra words to play at any time. Practice games do not affect your daily streak.

Does WordSprint Daily work on mobile?

Yes. Play with the on-screen keyboard on a phone or tablet, or type on a physical keyboard on a computer.
