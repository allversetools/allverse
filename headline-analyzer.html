    Headline Analyzer — FAQs

    What is a good headline score?

    70+ is strong, 50–70 is average and can be improved, below 50 needs work. Aim for balanced word types, ~6 words and a number.

    Why does word balance matter?

    Headlines mixing common words (readers skim these), uncommon words (catch attention), emotional words (drive clicks) and power words (prompt action) consistently outperform plain ones.

    Should my headline include a number?

    Yes — headlines with numbers get noticeably higher click-through. Odd numbers like 7 or 9 tend to perform best.

    How long should a headline be?

    Around 6 words and under 60 characters — long enough to promise value, short enough to display fully in search and social feeds.
